Sara Hamzeloui

Semiconductor Research Scientist

III–V device and integrated circuit design and process development

My work focuses on III–V semiconductor devices and integrated circuits for high-speed communications and millimeter-wave applications.

My work spans the complete technology development cycle: III–V device design and cleanroom fabrication, MMIC process integration and process design kits, circuit design, and advanced RF characterization. I have more than eight years of research experience in ETH Zurich’s Millimeter-Wave Electronics group, where I completed my PhD and now work as a postdoctoral researcher on InP HBT technology for high-speed optical transmitters. My doctoral thesis received the 2025 ETH Medal.

I earned my bachelor’s and master’s degrees in electrical engineering at Sharif University of Technology. My work has involved joint projects with academic and industry institutions including the University of Toronto (Prof Sorin Voinigescu’s Lab), CEA-Leti (France), Fraunhofer EMFT (Germany), and UCLouvain (Belgium). These collaborations have led to record-breaking circuit performances and have given me broad experience in coordinating semiconductor fabrication activities across multi-institutional research projects.

RESEARCH & ENGINEERING

From the Device to the Measured Circuit

Device
Development

I design and fabricate InP-based heterojunction bipolar transistors for millimeter-wave power amplifiers. My work covers epitaxial structure design, device layout, and process development, with a focus on power density, efficiency, and breakdown voltage. I use optical and electron-beam lithography, dry etching, and thin-film deposition to translate designs into devices. Results include 14.5 dBm output power at 94 GHz and 35% power-added efficiency at 170 GHz.

MMIC Technology
& PDK Development

I developed an InP DHBT MMIC fabrication process and the process design kit needed to design circuits in that technology. This includes active and passive device models, design rules, automated design-rule checks, and technology documentation. I connect process development with measured device behavior so circuit designs can be fabricated and evaluated consistently. The platform has supported broadband amplifier demonstrations and the integration of designs from collaborating research groups.

Integrated
Circuit Design

I design millimeter-wave power amplifiers and high-speed integrated circuits using InP HBT technology. Demonstrated circuits include a 94 GHz four-way combined power amplifier with 19.2 dBm output power. In a recent collaboration with University of Toronto, I fabricated a record-performance 357 GS/s analog multiplexer, designed by UofT graduate students.

RF & Millimeter-Wave
Characterization

I set up, calibrate, and operate on-wafer measurement systems to characterize semiconductor devices and integrated circuits. My experience includes DC measurements, S-parameters up to 300 GHz, large-signal load-pull measurements up to 170 GHz, and linearity measurements up to 50 GHz, as well as noise characterization. I use these measurements to evaluate power, efficiency, and linearity, validate compact models, and guide the next iteration of device and circuit design.
